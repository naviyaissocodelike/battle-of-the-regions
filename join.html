Access to capitalBeyond the cash prizes

District Angels is opening SPVs for select participating companies, giving them more investor exposure and access to capital after the competition.

Sponsor engagementGroundVue × J.P. Morgan

DC regional champion GroundVue opened a bank account with J.P. Morgan after learning about its startup banking offerings through its sponsorship of Battle of the Regions.

Regional championsTorev Motors, Arlington County

Torev won the Virginia regional, advanced to the championship and took second place, bringing home $2,500 in non-dilutive capital.

Founders also made connections with prospective customers, banks, economic development organizations, coworking spaces, law firms, service providers and investors.

For sponsors

Why sponsors back the championship

01Non-dilutive capital for founders

Provide non-dilutive capital to Seed and Series A startups across DC, Maryland, and Virginia, directly funding the region's most promising companies.

02Founder access & resources

Connect founders with capital, mentorship, and resources from a curated regional network of angels, VCs, and LPs.

03Ecosystem visibility

Increase visibility for active angels, institutional VCs, and ecosystem organizations operating across the DMV.

04Regional investor collaboration

Build cross-border investor collaboration across DC, Maryland, and Virginia, reinforcing regional cohesion.

05Co-investment opportunities

Create syndication, co-investment, and deal flow opportunities among diverse investor groups that are thesis- and stage-aligned.

06Community impact

Inspire university students and young professionals to build, join, and scale high-growth companies in the DMV ecosystem.
